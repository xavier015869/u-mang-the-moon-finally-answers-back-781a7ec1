The Moon finally answers back — one line, in the voice of the Moon replying
at last to the wishes, the howls and the late-night promises of everyone
staring up at it.

`main.ts` (Node 22, TypeScript, stdlib only) writes the entry to
`/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`), with a loud
failure if the line ever falls outside the 1–240 character gate or stops
being a single line.

Entry: "You wish on me, you howl at me, you promise you'll change. Four
billion years of this, and I have never dispensed a single thing — I'm a
rock, not a vending machine."
